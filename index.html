Concrete First Principles

This platform was developed by Dr. X.Y. Lu with the assistance of LLMs. It models concrete from first principles — drawing on cement chemistry, particle packing, suspension rheology, fracture mechanics, and corrosion electrochemistry — through eight axioms, twenty-four governing equations with parameter tables and a provenance ledger, and a working simulator that chains mix design, fresh-state properties, mechanics, fibers, and four durability arenas into twenty-five computed figures. It is intended not only as a working memo, but as an open invitation: those who find it promising are warmly welcome to enrich and extend it (see How to Contribute).

Concrete Design & Multi-Physics Simulator

Chain: mix → grading (M19) → rheology (M15+M17) → admixtures (M9/M10) → multi-blend SCM (M16) → mineralogy/gypsum (M18) → strength + fibers (M14/M17) → fracture (M7 skin two-threshold) → durability: frost (M10), corrosion (M20 skin profile), ASR/AAR (M21), sulfate (M22). Default skin = POROUS. Inputs re-run automatically on every change (0.25 s debounce).
